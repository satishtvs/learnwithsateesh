- Mean is the sum divided by N; variance is the average squared distance from the mean; standard deviation is its square root.
- Covariance shows whether two variables rise together; correlation divides it by both standard deviations and lies in -1 to +1.
- Gaussian: bell curve set by mean and variance; about 68.27% of values lie within 1 sigma, 95.45% within 2, 99.73% within 3.
- A Gaussian mixture is `p(x) = sum of pi_k N(x | mu_k, sigma_k^2)`, with weights pi_k that add up to 1.
- The component that produced each point is a hidden (latent) variable; its responsibility is the probability for each component.
- EM: E step computes responsibilities, M step re-estimates means, variances and weights; log-likelihood never decreases.
- EM reaches only a local maximum, so restart from several initial guesses and keep variances from collapsing.
- Choose the number of components K with BIC (lower is better); K = 2 was best in the example. Exam: write both EM steps.
