- A concept is a yes or no function over objects; concept learning infers it from labelled positive and negative examples.
- A hypothesis is an AND of one constraint per attribute: a specific value, ? (any value) or the empty set (no value).
- EnjoySport has 96 possible days, 5120 syntactic hypotheses and 973 semantically distinct ones (972 plus one all-negative).
- h1 is more general than h2 if every instance h2 accepts is also accepted by h1; this is only a partial order.
- Find-S starts with the most specific hypothesis and generalises just enough to cover each positive example.
- Find-S ignores negative examples completely.
- EnjoySport trace gives the final answer <Sunny, Warm, ?, Strong, ?, ?>: humidity, water and forecast become ? when positive examples disagree.
- Limits of Find-S: it returns one answer, cannot say if it is the only fit, and cannot detect noisy data.
- Exam takeaway: write Find-S and trace it on a table, changing h only on positive examples.
