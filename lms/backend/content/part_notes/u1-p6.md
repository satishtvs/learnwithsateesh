- Regression predicts a continuous target with a model such as y = b0 + b1 x, where b0 is the intercept and b1 the slope.
- A residual is the vertical gap t - y between a point and the line.
- Least squares minimises the sum of squared residuals; squaring stops errors cancelling and punishes big misses.
- Setting both derivatives to zero gives b1 = sum (x - xbar)(t - tbar) / sum (x - xbar)^2 and b0 = tbar - b1 xbar.
- The fitted line always passes through (xbar, tbar); for x = 1..5, t = 2,4,5,4,5 it is y = 2.2 + 0.6x.
- Matrix form: b = (X^T X)^-1 X^T t, with a column of ones in X for the intercept.
- Regression can classify by coding classes as 0 and 1 and thresholding at 0.5, but outliers can shift the boundary badly.
- Unlike the perceptron, regression has a direct formula, but it also fails on XOR.
