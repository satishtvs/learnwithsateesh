- Crossover mixes two parents: one-point swaps tails after a cut, two-point swaps the middle part, uniform decides each gene by coin toss.
- Mutation flips each bit with a small probability; it restores genes that crossover alone can never create.
- Too little mutation gives premature convergence, too much turns the search into a random walk.
- Remedies for premature convergence: higher mutation, bigger population, weaker selection pressure, rank selection, elitism.
- Elitism copies the best one or two strings unchanged, so the best fitness never goes down.
- Knapsack: 15-bit chromosome, fitness is value minus a penalty per unit of overweight; the optimum value was 286.
- Travelling salesman: use a permutation chromosome with order crossover and swap mutation, and fitness as negative tour length.
- A GA can also learn network weights (9 real numbers for a 2-2-1 XOR network) without gradients, but back-propagation is faster when gradients exist.
