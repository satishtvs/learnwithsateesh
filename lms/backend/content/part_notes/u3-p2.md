- CART makes only binary splits and builds both classification trees (class at the leaf) and regression trees (number at the leaf).
- Gini impurity `G = 1 - sum(p_i^2)`: 0 for a pure node, 0.5 for two classes in equal parts, no logarithm needed.
- Best split: try thresholds midway between sorted values and choose the lowest weighted Gini over all features.
- Worked example: `study <= 3.5` gives weighted Gini 0.1667, the best of eleven candidate splits.
- A regression leaf predicts the mean of its targets; the split with the largest variance reduction wins.
- Every cut is parallel to an axis, so a tree splits the input space into rectangles.
- A fully grown tree overfits (train 1.000, test 0.847 at 19 leaves); pre-pruning stops early, post-pruning cuts back.
- Cost complexity pruning minimises error + alpha times the number of leaves; choose alpha on validation data.
